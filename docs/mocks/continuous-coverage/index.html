Coverage timeline

Two different clocks, one per axis. Horizontally, each bar spans the service dates that dataset covers — that is where overlaps and gaps live. Vertically is real time: when we downloaded each one, newest at the top. So the seal, probation and today are all horizontal, because they happen in real time, not in service time. The vertical distance between two bars is the real gap between publications.

The green rule joining two bars is the service they share; a red block is service that no dataset covers. Drag or scroll sideways to zoom the service axis; hover anything for its numbers.
